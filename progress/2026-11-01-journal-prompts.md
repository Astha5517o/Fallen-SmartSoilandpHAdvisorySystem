# Journal prompts — 1 November 2026

Use these questions to write today's entry in your own words. This is deliberately a prompt sheet, not a completed journal.

## Start with what really happened
- What did I work on today?
- What made me draw this workflow/circuit diagram?
- Which parts did I include: sensor, ESP32, threshold, alert, power, LED/buzzer?
- Did I read anything or only sketch the idea? Be specific.

## My thinking
- What do I think the device should do for a farmer?
- Why did I choose to explore a capacitive sensor?
- What part of the drawing am I least sure about?
- What did I realise I need to verify before wiring?

## Be honest about progress
- What is only a plan right now?
- What have I actually completed?
- Did I build or test any hardware today? If not, say that plainly.

## Next step
- What is the smallest useful thing I can do next: identify exact component models, read datasheets, ask a mentor about the custom-PCB requirement, or revise the sketch?

## Optional closing sentence
Finish with one honest sentence about how you feel about the project today, and one thing you want to understand better tomorrow.
