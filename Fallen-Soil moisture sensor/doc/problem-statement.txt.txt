What Problem?
The crop need to be irrigated when they actually needed it,
If they are overirrigated or underirrigated the plant growth will be 
affected and also the soil nutrients also get disturbed by this. In
some conditions the soil erosion is also a factor of problem could
be happened.
Who has it?
Our farmers usually faces this problem as they use a fix time to irrigate
their plants but temperature and seasons factor could differ which
can cause the problems such as over or underirrigation. But farmer
usually have a busy time and tight budget so they will not be able to look 
led alert or do irrigation according to the different temperatures and
different seasons. 
Why does it matter??
If the farmer has such a busy schedule and low budget so he could have
normal mobile connecting alert system to help him to know best time
to irrigate their plants and further how much he should irrigate this
is not only benificial for farmers, the soil quality will also be 
benifited by it as the nutrients will get time to grew and other earthwarms
and fertilizers could really work more well with the help of this devise.
What my device do?
It will be under the soil and have sensors to detect moisture in soil 
near the root of plants and when the moisture is low or high or 
intermidiate it will give different status or alerts to the farmers
mobile as he could know best time to irrigate his plants.
